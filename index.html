Lens A · Behavioral Engine [EVIDENCE]

What we measure (Lens A)

Pressure is operationalised as an observable match state: required run rate, wickets in hand, balls remaining, innings phase and scoreboard leverage. A player is compared strictly against their own baseline with shrinkage priors and holdout validation.

Lens B · Interpretive Reading [INTERPRETATION]

What we interpret (Lens B)

Pressure is read as how a player relates to the burden — archetype, motif, defence pattern and repetition. It surfaces the psychological texture behind the state without claiming statistical proof. Reading confidence is capped at LOW or MEDIUM (never HIGH).

Epistemic Boundary[INTERPRETATION]

Lens B is not validated

The interpretive read is a story, not a measurement, and is never scored on holdout. Archetypes and motifs exist to frame human discussion in the auction and coaching room; they carry zero weight in the numerical reliability index and are capped at LOW or MEDIUM reading confidence.

07 · Methodology

Model, not magic.

The scientific layer is deliberately visible. Every major decision can be traced back to a pressure state, baseline, evidence and robustness test.

“The numbers never borrow authority from the story. The story never borrows authority from the numbers.”Methodological Separation
01
Define pressure [EVIDENCE]

Required run rate, wickets, balls remaining, innings phase and score context combine into pressure states rather than a single arbitrary “clutch” label.

02
Compare against normal [EVIDENCE]

Pressure performance is compared against an appropriate player baseline with population priors and shrinkage to protect small samples.

03
Build Pressure DNA [EVIDENCE]

Chase, defend, high-RRR, death and extreme-pressure behavior remain visible as separate fingerprints.

04
Reliability + uncertainty [EVIDENCE]

Pressure performance, rank consistency, evidence, robustness and uncertainty combine into a decision reliability index.

05
Validate on unseen seasons [EVIDENCE]

The ranking pipeline is evaluated season-by-season without using the held-out season for calibration.

Reliability = pressure performance + pressure score + consistency + evidence + robustness
The final UI presents the decision layer; the underlying data pipeline remains the source of truth.
Dual-Lens Epistemology[INTERPRETATION]

What this model is — and isn't

This model is behavioural, psychometric and decision-theoretic in method. It operationalises pressure as an observable match state and measures a player's response against their own baseline. It does not model a player's inner experience.

The interpretive lens is a READING, not a measurement. It borrows the vocabulary of depth psychology — archetype, defence, repetition — to make sense of patterns the numbers cannot explain. It is deliberately not validated, and the interface says so everywhere it appears.

Where the two lenses agree, confidence rises. Where they conflict, the conflict is the finding.
