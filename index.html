06 · Methodology

Model, not magic.

The scientific layer is deliberately visible. Every major decision can be traced back to a pressure state, baseline, evidence and robustness test.

01
Define pressure

Required run rate, wickets, balls remaining, innings phase and score context combine into pressure states rather than a single arbitrary “clutch” label.

02
Compare against normal

Pressure performance is compared against an appropriate player baseline with population priors and shrinkage to protect small samples.

03
Build Pressure DNA

Chase, defend, high-RRR, death and extreme-pressure behavior remain visible as separate fingerprints.

04
Reliability + uncertainty

Pressure performance, rank consistency, evidence, robustness and uncertainty combine into a decision reliability index.

05
Validate on unseen seasons

The ranking pipeline is evaluated season-by-season without using the held-out season for calibration.

Reliability = pressure performance + pressure score + consistency + evidence + robustness
The final UI presents the decision layer; the underlying data pipeline remains the source of truth.
